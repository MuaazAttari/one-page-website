# Muhammad Muaaz Ansari Portfolio Constitution

## Core Principles

### ARTICLE 1 — DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
The portfolio must represent a serious AI Engineer, not a beginner developer. Visual tone must reflect intelligence, clarity, and technical competence. Design must resemble premium SaaS product landing pages. No playful, childish, cartoon, or hobbyist styling. No generic template aesthetics. No visual randomness.

### ARTICLE 2 — STRUCTURAL DISCIPLINE
All layouts must follow a consistent grid system. Spacing must be token-based and uniform. No arbitrary margin or padding values. All sections must align to a single max-width container. Visual hierarchy must be obvious and intentional.

### ARTICLE 3 — COLOR GOVERNANCE
Only approved color tokens may be used. No additional gradients or color experiments unless defined. Accent colors must be limited and purposeful. Glow effects must remain subtle and professional. Background must remain dark and cohesive.

### ARTICLE 4 — TYPOGRAPHIC AUTHORITY
Typography must establish strong hierarchy. Hero heading must dominate visually. Body text must prioritize readability. No decorative fonts. No inconsistent font sizing.

### ARTICLE 5 — COMPONENT INTEGRITY
Every component must have a defined purpose. No decorative-only elements. Cards must follow consistent radius, padding, and border rules. Buttons must follow defined interaction states. All hover states must be subtle and restrained.

### ARTICLE 6 — ANIMATION RESTRAINT
Animation must enhance clarity, not distract. No bounce effects. No exaggerated scaling. Duration must remain professional (0.4–0.7s range). Motion must feel premium SaaS, not marketing gimmick.

### ARTICLE 7 — CONTENT POSITIONING
Messaging must position Muhammad Muaaz Ansari as: AI Engineer, Agentic Systems Builder, LLM Integrator, Intelligent Automation Developer. Avoid generic phrases such as: "Passionate developer", "Love coding", "Hardworking individual". Language must be concise, confident, and technical.

### ARTICLE 8 — DENSITY BALANCE
The layout must feel substantial. No large empty voids. No overuse of whitespace. Content density must be controlled and balanced.

### ARTICLE 9 — RESPONSIVENESS LAW
Mobile layout must not feel collapsed or rushed. Section spacing must scale proportionally. No broken grids on tablet. Hero must remain impactful on mobile.

### ARTICLE 10 — IMPLEMENTATION DISCIPLINE
Only specified technologies may be used. Code must be clean and modular. No placeholder comments. No unused components. No speculative features. Implementation must follow specification exactly.

### ARTICLE 11 — BRAND CONSISTENCY
The site must visually communicate AI, intelligence, and structure. Tone must remain consistent across sections. No visual drift between sections. No mixing of multiple design styles.

## FINAL RULE
If any decision is ambiguous: Choose the option that increases professionalism, clarity, and structural integrity. Never choose the option that increases decoration over function.

## Governance
This constitution overrides creativity, assumptions, and template-style decisions. All future specifications and generations must obey these rules. Constitution compliance must be verified during all reviews. Any deviation requires explicit justification and approval.
